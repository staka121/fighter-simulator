# Privacy Policy — Fighter HUD

_Last updated: 2026-08-30_

Fighter HUD ("the app") is a heads-up display for Even Realities G2 smart
glasses. The app runs on your phone inside the Even app and renders symbology on
the glasses. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes.

## Summary

- The developer does **not** collect, store, or transmit your personal data to
  any server we control.
- Destinations ("goals") you create are stored **locally on your device** and
  are not sent to us.
- The app uses your **precise location** on-device to drive the display, and
  contacts two third-party services (maps and elevation) to provide navigation
  features, as described below.
- No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no AI/ML.

## Location

The app uses your device's location (GPS) to display ground speed, heading,
altitude, your current position, and navigation to goals. Location is processed
on your device and is not sent to the developer.

## Third-party services

To provide the map and elevation features, the app makes requests to the
following services. We do not control these services; their own privacy policies
apply.

- **OpenStreetMap** — map tiles are loaded to display the map on the phone.
  Because the map is centered on your current location, tile requests reveal the
  approximate area you are viewing to OpenStreetMap's tile servers.
  - <https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright>
  - <https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy>
- **Open-Meteo** — when you pick or enter a goal, that goal's coordinates are
  sent to the Open-Meteo Elevation API to look up ground elevation.
  - <https://open-meteo.com/>
  - <https://open-meteo.com/en/terms>

## Data storage and retention

Goals (coordinates and an optional altitude) are stored in local storage on your
device. They remain until you delete them in the app or clear the app's data.
You can remove goals at any time from the app.

## Permissions

- **Location** — required for the HUD's speed, heading, altitude, position, and
  navigation.
- **Background execution** (optional) — lets the HUD keep running while you walk
  with the phone in your pocket.

## Children

The app is not directed to children.
